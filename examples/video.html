VARZ components Slider Lightbox Video

Video

A YouTube or Vimeo embed loads 0.5-1 MB of scripts before anyone presses play. varz-video.js shows a poster link instead and loads the player only on click. Nothing is requested from YouTube or Vimeo until then - without JavaScript the link opens the video page.

Self-hosted video

A link to a video file (.mp4, .webm, .ogv, .mov) plays in the browser's own player - nothing from a third party. The link's image becomes the video poster, so there is no black flash while the file loads. data-vz-captions adds a WebVTT captions track, shown by default.

Videos in a slider

A video stops when its slide leaves (a file pauses, YouTube / Vimeo return to the poster). The first slide has data-vz-autoplay: it starts muted when the slide comes in. The slider's own autoplay waits while a video plays.

Photos and videos in the lightbox

A data-varz-lightbox link to a video page or file plays the video in the viewer. Moving to the next item or closing stops it.

AttributeWhat it does
data-varz-videoOn click the link is replaced by the player (same style attribute), which autoplays and gets the focus.
data-vz-titlePlayer title for screen readers; without it the poster's alt text is used.
data-vz-autoplayStarts the video muted when its container is activated - a slide coming into view, a tab or accordion panel opening (varz.js), or a wrapper with data-vz-event="inview". A playing video always stops when its container is deactivated.
data-vz-captionsVideo files: a WebVTT captions file (.vtt), shown by default. data-vz-captions-lang - its language (default: the page language), data-vz-captions-label - its name in the player menu (default "Captions").
hrefYouTube (watch, youtu.be, shorts, embed links; ?t= start time - played from youtube-nocookie.com), Vimeo (also unlisted links), or a video file: .mp4, .webm, .ogv, .mov.

Ctrl / Cmd-click opens the video page as a normal link. The poster is your own image - a YouTube thumbnail URL would contact Google before the click. Texts: varz.video.i18n. varz.media.parse( url ) is shared with varz-lightbox.js.
