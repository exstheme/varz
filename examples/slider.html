Slider

A native horizontal scroller with CSS scroll-snap - swipe, trackpad and keyboard work without any script. varz-slider.js (3.8 kB minified + gzipped) adds arrows, dots, mouse drag, autoplay with a pause button, loop and accessible labels.

Control looks

Generated controls (data-vz-controls) in six dot looks and four arrow looks - bar puts the arrows next to the dots.

CSS only (no script)

The same track without data-varz-slider: swipe, trackpad or Shift + mouse wheel; visible scrollbar.

Markup

<div data-varz-slider data-vz-autoplay="5000" data-vz-loop aria-label="Featured">
  <div data-varz-track style="--d:flex;--ox:auto;--sst:x mandatory;--sbw:none;">
    <div style="--flb:100%;--fls:0;--ssa:start;">Slide 1</div>
    <div style="--flb:100%;--fls:0;--ssa:start;">Slide 2</div>
  </div>
  <button type="button" data-varz-prev>&lsaquo;</button>
  <button type="button" data-varz-next>&rsaquo;</button>
  <div data-varz-dots>
    <button type="button" data-varz-dot hidden style="--bgc:gray;--vz-bgc:red;"></button>
  </div>
  <button type="button" data-varz-play hidden>...</button>
</div>

AttributeOnWhat it does
data-varz-sliderwrapperTurns the block into a carousel (role region, aria-roledescription carousel).
data-vz-autoplay="ms"wrapperNext slide every ms. Pauses on hover, focus and in hidden tabs; stops for good after a swipe or the pause button. Off with prefers-reduced-motion.
data-vz-loopwrapperArrows wrap around at the ends (autoplay always does).
data-vz-controls="arrows,dots,play"wrapperAdds the controls the markup does not have, with default looks (varz.slider.styles) - a bare track becomes a full slider.
data-vz-dots="pill"wrapperLook of generated dots: pill (default, the active one wider), round, square, line, ring, number.
data-vz-arrows="round"wrapperLook of generated arrows: round (default), square, outline, plain; add bar to place them next to the dots.
data-varz-dot-numberinside the dot templateGets the dot's number (numbered dots in your own markup).
data-varz-trackscrollerThe scroll-snap container; its children are the slides (labelled "1 of 4"). Focusable: ← → keys.
data-varz-prev / data-varz-nextbuttonPrevious / next position, disabled at the ends without loop.
data-varz-dots + data-varz-dotcontainer + templateOne dot per reachable position (fewer than slides when several are in view). Active dot: .vz, aria-current.
data-varz-playbuttonPause / play; shown only with autoplay; .vz while playing.

.vz is added to the slides in view (style with --vz-*), events: varz-slide on the wrapper, varz-activated / varz-deactivated on slides. Scroll props from VARZ: --sst scroll-snap-type, --ssa scroll-snap-align, --sbw scrollbar-width (responsive), --osb overscroll-behavior, --lst list-style, --foc-olo focus outline offset.
